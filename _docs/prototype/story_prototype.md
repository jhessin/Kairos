# Kairos Prototype Story

## Working Title

Kairos — Capernaum

## Target Playtime

30–45 minutes

## Story Purpose

The prototype introduces:

1. The Kairos Initiative
2. Synchronization
3. Historical exploration
4. The player's counterpart
5. Words of Power
6. Macros
7. Archaeology
8. Quantum Anchors
9. The larger mystery

---

## ACT I — THE EXCAVATION

### Scene 1 — The Excavation

Miriam Halevi and Evelyn Saroyan discover an ancient chamber.

Miriam detects an unusual quantum disturbance.

Miriam touches an artifact.

FLASH.

She experiences the ancient world.

An unknown girl calls her "Mary."

Miriam returns to the present.

### Scene 2 - The Team

Halevi and Saroyan meet up with Dr. Marcus Ellington and Prof. Daniel Kessler to
discuss how to recreate Halevi's experience.

There is a bit of discussion about how Halevi synchronized with another
version of her from the 'past' on a quantum level.

Kessler postulates that her mind could not continue to synchronize with her
counterpart because her mind and customs couldn't understand what she was experiencing.

Kessler and Ellington have been working on building a quantum VR computer that
can interface with the human mind and they postulate that they can create a
buffer interface that would prevent this from happening in the future. However,
Halevi cannot go back because she was so abruptly de-synchronized.

Kessler: The bottom line is we need someone else that resonates with the
artifact - a Resonant if you will.

---

## ACT II — THE KAIROS INITIATIVE

The player is introduced to the Kairos Initiative.

It can be skipped for the prototype, but the full game should have a
questionnaire that the player fills out that narrows down their counterpart to
one of several characters they can take on the role of.

The player learns:

- What synchronization is
- Why their brain scan indicated they have the potential to be a Resonant
- Why the ancient world is being investigated
- What the player will be doing
- That their historical counterpart is important
- How their VR HUD will display information that will help them stay in sync
  with their counterpart

---

## ACT III — CAPERNAUM

The player synchronizes with the ancient world.

The player:

1. Arrives in Capernaum
2. Explores the village
3. Meets Eliab - A friend who was expecting them and Mara - His wife.
4. Learns about their counterpart
5. Helps with a local problem

---

## ACT IV — DISCOVERY

The player:

1. Collects materials
2. Crafts a simple object under the guidance of Yosef the local craftsman
3. Encounters hostile NPCs
4. Discovers an archaeological site
5. Finds an ancient inscription
6. Learns the first Word of Power

---

## ACT V — THE ANCHOR

The player discovers an unusual artifact.

The artifact appears connected to the modern archaeological site.

The synchronization begins destabilizing.

The player is forced to return.

---

## ACT VI — RETURN

The player wakes in the Kairos facility.

The researchers examine the recovered information.

The artifact exists in both realities.

The prototype ends.

---

## Prototype Ending

Miriam:

"We know where we're going next."

CUT TO BLACK.
