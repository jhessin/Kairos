# Kairos Issues

## Need Solving

1. How do we deal with historical treatment of sensitive topics such as
   sexism/racism?

   - We want to maintain historical accuracy, but we don't want to blatantly
     offend those who play the game. For example there weren't any female warrior
     types in the biblical era - but if a player wants to play as a female it
     wouldn't do to exclude them from combat entirely.

   - One possible solution is to have the quantum synchronization be explained
     so that even though modern player is female they may find themselves playing
     a male counterpart in the past. I'm not sure how this translates in quantum
     mechanics but if we need to give in an area I would rather it be here than
     historical accuracy or player experience.
