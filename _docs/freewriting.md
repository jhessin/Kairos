# Free-writing

## Musings

I don't have a solid antagonist. I want the combat system to feel like an
integral part of the game, but nothing so far has tied into that. With that in
mind we are going to go into some crazy stuff!

What if the player is going into the past to keep the present in tact. God has a
plan for humanity - the whole salvation story. The devil is trying to stop it in
any way he can. Demons (including ancient gods such as Zeus, Hades, etc.) are
trying to stop prophecies from happening and you as the player need to set it right.

To help you there are Words of Power and Macro Grids that are hidden throughout
history. You can find those and use them to create different miracles - not the
same ones as those recorded in the bible but similar to those. This gives some
credence to the doubters that there is a logical explanation for biblical
miracles - but the more you learn the more you are pointed to a creator that
made the Words of Power to begin with.

As the player de-synchronizes they are found by the Enemy who sends Demons to
pull him out of his counterpart. This creates a combat zone that is isolated
from the other people and limits how much damage they can do.

The player therefore is trying to keep the timeline in order but also keep from
being noticed. An ability that the player should unlock relatively early is the
ability to see other quantum travelers like themselves. The player should
believe that the Enemy is another quantum traveler at the start and then as the
story unfolds they find that they are spiritual enemies.

## Concerns

With all the metaphysical stuff I want to be sure not to diverge from the
biblical purpose of the game. As a main concern many Christians may not
understand the spiritual battle and the parallel that the game draws between
that and the "Grey Zone" (the de-synchronized world the player finds themselves
in).
