# Timeline

> **Build one polished 30–45 minute playable story experience that proves Kairos is fun.**

The Capernaum prototype is a solo vertical slice. Its story is organized as a short quest chain that introduces one gameplay concept at a time while establishing the larger Resonance mystery and giving the player a clear personal objective: find Miriam and bring her back if possible.

## Prototype Experience

```text
Modern Kairos Facility
        ↓
Opening Excavation
        ↓
Miriam's First Synchronization
        ↓
Miriam falls into a coma / consciousness remains with counterpart
        ↓
Evelyn brings in Daniel Kessler and Marcus Ellington
        ↓
Player Introduction / Kairos Briefing
        ↓
Synchronize with Eliab
        ↓
Q001 — A Stranger in Eliab's Life
        ↓
Q002 — The Master's Errand
        ↓
Q003 — A Friend's Challenge
        ↓
Q004 — A Wife's Question
        ↓
Q005 — Something Is Wrong
        ↓
Q006 — Into the Grey
        ↓
Q007 — The Word
        ↓
Q008 — The Anchor
        ↓
Q009 — Return
        ↓
Modern Kairos Facility
        ↓
New lead for finding Miriam / Next Zone Tease
```

## Prototype Definition of Done

The finished slice should demonstrate:

- Modern Kairos hub/facility
- Opening Resonance discovery
- Miriam's coma and trapped consciousness as the initial story hook
- Player as a Resonant
- Eliab as the player's counterpart
- Capernaum as one historical zone
- Living as Eliab as the central narrative problem
- Searching for clues to Miriam's location/condition
- Dialogue choices
- Sync Stability
- Gathering
- One crafting activity
- Combat training
- One historical hostile encounter
- Desynchronization
- Grey Zone
- One Demon encounter
- Archaeological discovery
- At least two Words of Power
- One usable Macro Grid
- Quantum Anchor
- Emergency extraction
- Return to Kairos
- A new lead toward Miriam
- Prototype cliffhanger

## Scope

| Category | Prototype |
|---|---|
| Modern hub | 1 small Kairos facility area |
| Historical zone | 1 Capernaum area |
| Grey Zone | 1 small combat space |
| Playtime | 30–45 minutes |
| Quest chain | 8 primary quests + return sequence |
| NPCs | Small focused cast |
| Historical enemies | 1 small encounter |
| Demons | 1 type |
| Macro | 1 usable Macro |
| Words | 2–3 |
| Archaeology | 1 simplified discovery |
| Crafting | 1 pottery activity |
| Multiplayer | No |
| MMO persistence | No |

## Development Schedule

### Week 1 — Production / Story Lock

- Lock prototype premise
- Lock Eliab as counterpart
- Lock Miriam's coma / trapped-consciousness hook
- Lock quest chain
- Lock Grey Zone role
- Lock first Words and Macro
- Lock prototype ending
- Establish backlog and scope

**Deliverable:** stable Lesson 10 baseline plus locked prototype design.

### Week 2 — Player Controller

- Movement polish
- Camera polish
- Dodge/run
- Gamepad support

**Deliverable:** player feels good to control.

### Week 3 — Interaction

- NPC interaction
- Object interaction
- Interaction prompts

**Deliverable:** consistent interaction framework.

### Week 4 — Dialogue

- Dialogue UI
- Speaker names
- Choices
- Events
- Synchronization effects

**Deliverable:** complete branching conversation.

### Week 5 — Quest Framework

- Quest state
- Objectives
- Completion
- Quest chain progression
- Rewards

**Deliverable:** Q001–Q002 playable through the framework.

### Week 6 — Gathering / Crafting

- Clay gathering
- Water/material gathering
- Simple inventory
- Pottery recipe

**Deliverable:** Q002 playable end-to-end.

### Week 7 — Combat

- Attack
- Combo
- Dodge
- Damage
- Death
- Enemy AI
- D-Pad targeting

**Deliverable:** Q003 combat encounter playable.

### Week 8 — Synchronization

- Sync Stability
- Choice modifiers
- Desynchronization threshold
- Quest restart behavior

**Deliverable:** player can intentionally fail and recover a quest through synchronization rules.

### Week 9 — Grey Zone

- Transition effect
- Isolated combat space
- Demon enemy
- Recovery / return

**Deliverable:** Q006 playable.

### Week 10 — Words and Macro

- Word discovery
- Word persistence
- Macro Grid
- Or ability
- Second Word data

**Deliverable:** Q007 playable with one usable Macro.

### Week 11 — Archaeology / Anchor

- Simplified discovery interaction
- Anchor creation/discovery
- Time-loop connection to opening artifact
- Miriam lead

**Deliverable:** Q008 playable.

### Week 12 — Prototype Integration

- Connect all quests
- Add transitions
- Add modern return
- Add final dialogue
- Add Miriam rescue hook / next-zone tease

**Deliverable:** complete beginning-to-end prototype.

### Weeks 13–16 — Polish

- Art pass
- Audio
- UI polish
- Animation
- Dialogue polish
- Bug fixing
- Performance

**Deliverable:** presentable vertical slice.

### Weeks 17–20 — Buffer / Finalization

- Playtesting
- Fixes
- Scope cuts if needed
- Packaging
- Final demo build

**Deliverable:** stable public-facing prototype.

## What Not to Build

Do not build for the prototype:

- Multiplayer
- MMO networking
- MMO persistence
- Guilds
- Trading
- Housing
- Multiple historical zones
- Full economy
- Large NPC simulation
- Large skill trees
- Large crafting trees
- Full language-learning system
- Complex archaeology simulation
- Large enemy roster
