# MMO Gameplay

Here is where we will describe the gameplay mechanics used in the game. This
includes how the player will travel to different time periods and locales as
well as how the games interface will work.

## Travel

Travel in the game will be done (in both time period and region) through finding
artifacts in the past that the present day NPC's will go find in the present as
the story progresses. One an artifact is found then the team can find a new
quantum anchor to send the player to.

## Controls

The controls should be usable through a gamepad. This will help in future
console development. Mouse and keyboard should be an option, but not needed for
gameplay. Dual thumb-stick movement and camera controls should be intuitive.

The gameplay should feel like an Action-RPG with immediate actions.

### Gamepad controls (Xbox Style)

- Left Stick:
  - Controls movement.

- L3 Button:
  - Click for dashing/running. (Toggle or hold in settings)

- Right Stick:
  - Controls the camera.

- R3 Button:
  - Click to toggle through different zoom factors including first-person.
    (These should be customizable in settings)

- A Button:
  - Jump/Confirm

- B Button:
  - Cancel/Hold to interact

- X Button:
  - Basic Attack/Quick Action
  - Different areas will have different quick actions. In combat centric areas
    this will be attack, but in other areas this may be 'talk', or 'interact'.

- Y Button:
  - Primary Macro Key
  - This is the unique button that the player will be able to program and bind
    to a certain action throughout the game. More about this in the
    [Macros](#macros) section.

- Start Button:
  - Opens the main menu.
  - Pauses the game in single player content, but shows a warning if the player
    is in a group environment stating the game is NOT paused.

- Select Button:
  - Toggle Mini-map size.
  - Toggles between mini/full-screen/hidden map modes.

- D-Pad:
  - By default this will be used to scroll through enemies on screen.
  - Also used to navigate menus.

- The shoulder buttons and triggers have yet to be determined.
  - Possible uses:
    - combo keys for quick actions.
    - Zelda-like z-targeting.
    - Alternative targeting methods?

## Macros

The Macro system is a major selling point for this game. Macros are user created
actions that are a combination of several factors.

As the user plays through the game they will find grids that they can use. They
may only use one grid at a time but may switch at any time outside of combat.

The grid will use a main stat such as Strength, Intelligence, Dexterity, or Luck
and add modifiers to it. They could create a strike(STR), a buff(INT), a ranged
attack(DEX), or a special attack(LUK) depending on what they place in the macro.
Each grid will have a different number of slots and different aspects or
requirements for each slot.

As players go through the Main Story Quest they will unlock different Hebrew
Words of Power that can be placed in these slots. These will not be tradable
with other players, but must be found by the player to be used.

## Leveling

The game will have several different forms of leveling. The player will have a
quantum level that will follow them from zone to zone. (A zone is a time-place
that the player syncs to: i.e. King David's Court). This level can never go
down. It will speed up the speed at which they gain sync levels, and also
determine the size of the grids that they will be able to use. [See macros.](#macros)
Skills will also unlock as quantum level increases.

They will also have a sync level which will be different for each zone. This
level will determine their stats as well as how many and which abilities can be
placed in their grids.

If a player dies they will lose their sync level and need to start over.
Whenever they first enter a zone they will choose a class that cannot be changed
in that zone unless they die, (or purposely re-sync as another character). In
either case they will reset their sync level.

The bottom line is that items and sync levels will be bound to a particular
zone, but skills and quantum levels will follow the player and never reset.
