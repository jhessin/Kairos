# Words of Power

Words of Power are persistent discoveries made during the Main Story Quest. Each Word should connect **language, historical context, gameplay function, and narrative meaning**.

They are not tradable and must be discovered by the player.

## Prototype Words

### אוֹר — Or

**Strong's:** H216

**Transliteration:** _ʾôr_

**Core concept:** light, illumination, dawn

**Prototype function:** Used by the first Macro to create an offensive light-infused attack and demonstrate the Macro system.

---

### חֹשֶׁךְ — Ḥōshekh

**Strong's:** H2822

**Transliteration:** _ḥōshekh_

**Core concept:** darkness, obscurity

**Prototype function:** Early-game second Word. Its final gameplay implementation can remain minimal, but it establishes that Words can represent different concepts and produce different Macro behavior.

> **Note:** H2822 is חֹשֶׁךְ (*ḥōshekh*), not קָדַשׁ. קָדַשׁ (*qāḏaš*, H6942) is a different Hebrew root meaning to be holy, sanctify, consecrate, or set apart.

---

## Prototype Rule

The prototype should demonstrate at least two Words without attempting to build the complete Word library.

Words should be discovered through story and exploration rather than purchased or randomly awarded.
